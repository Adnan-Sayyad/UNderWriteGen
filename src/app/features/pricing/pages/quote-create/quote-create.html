How the Pricing Engine Works
  1. Submission data is fetched — sum insured, product line, tenure, occupation type, and inception date.
  2. Risk score from the rules engine determines the risk band (Low / Medium / High / Unacceptable) and the corresponding loading %.
  3. Base premium is computed: SumInsured × BaseRate × TenureFactor
  4. Loadings (risk, occupation) are added on top of the base.
  5. Discounts (tenure, loyalty, preferred agent) are subtracted — only when Apply Discounts is enabled.
  6. GST at 18% is applied — only when Apply Taxes is enabled.
  7. The quote is saved in Draft status. Change status to Presented before the client can accept it.
