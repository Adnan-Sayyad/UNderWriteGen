About the Platform

Built for Modern Insurance Teams

UnderwritePro replaces fragmented spreadsheets and legacy systems with a unified, cloud-ready platform. Every module — from risk scoring to compliance audit — talks to each other through a secure API gateway, giving your team a single source of truth for every policy.

Microservices Architecture

11 independent services, each owning its domain, deployable and scalable independently without affecting the rest of the platform.

Role-Based Security

JWT-secured endpoints with 8 distinct roles — Admin, Underwriter, Agent, Compliance, Pricing, Operations, Assistant and more.

Full Audit Trail

Every action is logged. Regulators and internal auditors get a complete, tamper-evident history of every decision made on the platform.
